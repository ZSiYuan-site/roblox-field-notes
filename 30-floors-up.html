Entry: 30 Floors Up

Genre: survival. Studio: Dateline NBC. Players: up to 30, solo or with up to three friends. Created: 25 October 2026.

The goal is to survive 100 days in an apartment high above a zombie apocalypse. You scavenge food, store water and barricade the flat, build shelters on the roof, help your neighbours and uncover secrets. The game is free to play and the passes are optional. The shop listed ten passes when we checked on 6 November, and this entry sorts them by price.

The ten passes

  • Shove, 99 Robux: press H to push a player or zombie away. Players fall for 1.5 seconds with no damage, zombies stagger, and there is a 10 second cooldown.
  • Infinite Stamina, 249: sprinting never drains stamina, though running is still noisy.
  • Supporter, 299: a title and a tag above your head.
  • Custom Music Radio, 299: play your own audio on a battery radio. The infected hear it too.
  • Spoon, 499: a spoon in your first hotbar slot each match, which opens canned food without a can opener.
  • Lucille, 799: a barbed-wire baseball bat every match.
  • Deadweight, 999: a zombie head on a stick that hits like the free baseball bat.
  • Save Slot 2 and Save Slot 3, 999 each: extra independent campaign slots.
  • Locksmith, 1,299: a class that starts with a screwdriver and nine bobby pins.

Which ones change the run

Only a few passes change what you can do. Shove, Infinite Stamina, the Spoon and the Locksmith class matter in play; the rest are cosmetic, convenience or extra save slots. If you spend nothing, you lose nothing essential.

The free weapon question

The Baseball Bat has Damage 26 and Reach 8.0 on its item card, the highest of every card we read. The Hammer is 23 and 5.0, the Frying Pan 22 and 6.0, and the Kitchen Knife 18 and 4.5. Your hotbar has four slots, so do not carry three weapons. The 30 floors up best weapon page has the tier list.

Codes

There is no confirmed code box, and none of the shop tabs we saw had a field for one. Code lists for 18 Floors are a different game. See 30 floors up game passes, 30 floors up guide and the main 30 floors up roblox page. Back to the log.

Why the list is sorted by price

Sorting by price makes the trade-off visible. The cheapest pass, Shove, is also one of the few that changes how you handle a crowded hallway, while the most expensive one, Locksmith, changes your starting kit rather than your power. Lucille, Locksmith and the two save slots were not on the list we saw on 4 November, so the shop has already changed once since launch.
