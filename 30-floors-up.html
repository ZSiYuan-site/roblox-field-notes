Roblox Field Notes

Entry: 30 Floors Up

Genre: survival. Studio: Dateline NBC. Players: up to 30, solo or with up to three friends.

The goal is to survive 100 days in an apartment high above a zombie apocalypse. You scavenge food, store water and barricade the flat, build shelters on the roof, help your neighbours and uncover secrets. In the end you choose between waiting for rescue and risking a way out.

Things we noted

The shop sells ten game passes at the last check on 6 November, including Infinite Stamina and Shove, and passes such as Lucille and Locksmith appeared after our first look. The Baseball Bat outclasses the Frying Pan on damage and reach, which surprised us because we had only read the pan stats at first.

There is no confirmed code box. The lobby has Shop, Lobbies and Settings buttons, and none of the shop tabs we saw had a code field. Be wary of code lists for 18 Floors, which is a different game.
