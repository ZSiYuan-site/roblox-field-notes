30 Floors Up building map: where each floor sits and what is on it

30 Floors Up has 30 floors, and you start at the top: the apartment doors read 30xx (3002 is a neighbor's door). This note puts every floor named in the objectives in order, so you can see how the game moves you through the building. Everything below comes from objective text and captions in player videos; floors 1 to 10 and 12 to 18 have not been shown.

From top to bottom

  • Roof: the Day 28 objective. Open lift 2's landing door on 30, climb the shaft ladder, undo the machine room hatch with a wrench, go out onto the roof (roof and escape).
  • 30: your home and the hallway. A cork board, a barred gate, and a caption about slow pounding behind the 29 landing door.
  • 28: Arthur Hale's floor. Day 20 sends you down lift 2's shaft, then through the fire doors, to the maintenance room. Bring a hammer or screwdriver and a bandage.
  • 27: the management office. Day 31 to 32 objectives ask for Arthur's key or forcing the door, a wrench for the north tank riser valve, and cutting the chain on the fire doors below.
  • 26: a landing with a family note signed by the Haddads (2604). Stair A is burnt below here.
  • 19: June's community kitchen. Day 38 asks for two empty water containers and a fix to the Stair B barrier before Day 40.
  • 11: a clinic named in the Day 34 to 36 objective, not shown on screen.

Why the layout matters

Going down is a chain of small jobs, each naming its tool: key, wrench, hammer, board. Going up is the ladder in the lift shaft. The lift is numbered (lift 2), the stairs are lettered (Stair A, Stair B), and the 27 office's service door links the shaft to Stair B. If you remember only that, the later objectives on the later days page read much more clearly.

Two practical notes

First, the cutting of fire door chains is loud, and the objective itself says to draw zombies off first. Second, the footage does not say whether floors are fixed or change between campaigns, so treat this map as one run's route rather than a guarantee.
