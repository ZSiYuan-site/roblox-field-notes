Entry: Pocket Landlord

Genre: simulation and tycoon. Studio: UGDEVS (Underground Developers). Players: up to 6. Released: 20 October 2026.

The loop is gentle: decorate a room, find a tenant, collect the rent, build the next place. Tenants bring dogs, cats and bunnies. Walls and floors take any colour you like. Beyond apartments you can build lofts and villas, and then businesses such as a cafe, a gym or a karaoke bar. This entry focuses on how the level system works, because that is what decides what you can build next.

Where levelling happens

The top bar shows a level title with a goals counter. Pressing it opens the Community Prosperity window, which lists the goals for the next level and has a Celebrate and level up button. Below the goals, a Coming up section previews the next levels so you can see what you are working toward.

Titles seen on screen

Across the footage we noted Landlord, Block Captain, Neighbourhood Pride, Rising Landlord and Home Maker. These are the names shown next to the goals counter at different points in a run.

The goal list we could read

  • Block Captain to Rising Landlord: upgrade the Maple Utility Shed to level 2, rent out one Shared Apartment and run three businesses. It unlocks Shared Apartment 302, Chef tenants and a Bubble Wash slot, with a reward of 2,500 coins.
  • Prosperity 6 preview: Fitness Fan tenants, a Flex and Flow slot and Design Showdown.
  • Prosperity 7 preview: a landlord home and a Mint Clinic slot.
  • Prosperity 12: a goal asking for 500,000 coins in total, seen in late-game footage.

Signs in the world

Boards reading Coming soon with a Prosperity level next to them, and one room marked Opening soon, line up with the same system. If you see a locked room, the sign tells you which level opens it.

On codes

A lot of searches pair this game with the word codes. Our check on 8 November found nothing: the Roblox description lists none, no code-list site had a page for the game, and we could not confirm that a code box exists. Do not paste codes from Rent Please! Landlord Sim into this game. If the developer announces one, it will appear with a date on the pocket landlord roblox codes page.

Why the goal list matters

Each goal list doubles as a to-do list for the next hour of play, so reading it before you spend coins avoids buying things that do not help. For example, the Block Captain step asks for three businesses, so a fourth shop before then does nothing for your level.
