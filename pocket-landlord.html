Entry: Pocket Landlord

Genre: simulation and tycoon. Studio: UGDEVS (Underground Developers). Players: up to 6. Released: 20 October 2026.

The loop is gentle: decorate a room, find a tenant, collect the rent, build the next place. Tenants bring dogs, cats and bunnies. Walls and floors take any colour you like. Beyond apartments you can build lofts and villas, and then businesses such as a cafe, a gym or a karaoke bar. Levelling up unlocks new neighbourhoods.

On codes

A lot of searches pair this game with the word codes. Our check on 8 November found nothing: the Roblox description lists none, no code-list site had a page for the game, and we could not confirm that a code box exists. We did not want to guess where it is, so we say so plainly.

Do not paste codes from Rent Please! Landlord Sim into this game. They are separate games with similar themes.
