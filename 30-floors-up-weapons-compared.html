Why the Baseball Bat wins

It has the most damage and the longest reach of every card we read, so there is no trade-off to weigh. It was held on Day 8 in one run and stayed in the hotbar to Day 38 in a long run.

Hammer or Frying Pan

The Hammer hits one point harder (23 against 22) while the Frying Pan reaches one point farther (6.0 against 5.0). We put both in A tier. The Hammer is also a tool for objectives: the Day 12 east fire door and the Stair B barrier both ask for one, so you will likely carry it anyway.

Price per point

None of these are bought; they are found in apartments. That makes the real cost the time you spend searching, and the item cards are the only thing that lets you decide in the moment. Pick up anything with a Weapon tag, compare the two numbers, and drop the worse one to save space.

What this list does not know

We do not know durability or noise per weapon, and noise matters in a game where staying quiet is a big part of the plan. Until a video shows these, treat the tiers as damage-first, reach-second guidance only.

How the numbers were collected

Every figure above was read off an item card that was visible in a player video, with the video and the in-game day noted on the long page. When two videos disagreed we kept the one where the card was fully on screen. If a number is missing, as with the wrench's reach, it means the card was cut off or covered by the hotbar, not that the stat is zero. We would rather leave a blank than fill it in from memory of another game.
