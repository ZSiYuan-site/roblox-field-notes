Roblox Field Notes

Entry: +1 Spider Arm

Genre: incremental simulator. Studio: This Time For Sure. Players: up to 12. Released: 19 October 2026.

You ride a mechanical spider armature that walks, clings and climbs. Hold Space to climb walls, hang under ceilings and swing across gaps. Training raises your level, and levelling at set points gives you +1 arm, which means more speed and more grip. The final goal is the top of a lighthouse, after 13 stages.

Stage requirements compared

Each stage banner shows a recommended level and the grip it needs. The numbers below come from the banners in player footage, so they are a snapshot rather than a promise.

  • Stage 3, Boulder Canyon: level 5, 171 grip. Sandy ground with a few cacti.
  • Stage 4, Frost Spires: level 10, 250 grip. A grey wall, and the first place the OUT OF GRIP warning shows up.
  • Tsunami: level 14, 344 grip.
  • Twisted Causeway: level 25, 465 grip.
  • Honeycomb Hive: level 50, 859 grip.
  • Stage 13, Lighthouse Climb: level 120, 2,177 grip. A tall white and red wall with an arrow showing the way up.

Grip needs roughly double between level 25 and level 50, then more than double again by the lighthouse, so stopping to train is not optional.

Arms and rebirth

The arm counter tells you when the next arm arrives: at level 10 it showed five arms with the next at level 11, and at level 12 it showed six arms with the next at level 14. Late frames read 20 arms MAX, so the arm count appears to stop growing while levels keep climbing. Rebirth bonuses scale as well, from plus 50 percent at level 10 to plus 750 percent at level 100 and plus 1,400 percent at level 120.

Wins and armatures

Wins are paid at the end of a stage, for example a plus 3,750 Wins popup on Stage 10, and are spent on 12 armatures, the last one called GENESIS. A group reward chest asks you to join the studio's Roblox group first.

Codes

Four codes are reported as of 8 November: SPIDERARM, SECONDCHANCE, LUCKYSPIN and WEBFAMILY. They are entered from the Shop icon under CODES. Spider Army is a different game with different codes. What the lobby shows

The lobby is labelled TRAIN, UNLOCK and CLIMB. It has coloured training pads, a board called Most Rebirths and a row of display rigs. The left side of the HUD carries Rebirth, Shop, Daily and a free gift button. Yellow pads marked plus 10 Wins sit on the ground in early stages, and the screen shows x2 Train and x2 Wins boosts on the right.
