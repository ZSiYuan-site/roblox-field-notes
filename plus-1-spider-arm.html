Entry: +1 Spider Arm

Genre: incremental simulator. Studio: This Time For Sure. Players: up to 12.

You ride a mechanical spider armature that walks, clings and climbs. Hold Space to climb walls, hang under ceilings and swing across gaps. Training raises your level, and levelling gets you +1 arm, which means more speed and more grip.

Grip is the timer

Your grip drains while you hang. If it runs out, you fall. So the early game is a trade-off: go for the long hang across a gap, or stop on a ledge and recover. More arms stretch the timer.

Progress

There are 13 stages to escape, from rooftops and sand canyons to frost arches, lava, a haunted castle and a giant snake. Wins unlock 12 armatures, the last one called GENESIS, and Rebirth boosts your training. The final goal is the top of the lighthouse. The description also mentions free daily rewards.

Codes

Several codes are reported, among them SPIDERARM, SECONDCHANCE and LUCKYSPIN, entered from the Shop icon under CODES. Code lists change quickly, so check the date on our plus 1 spider arm codes page before pasting anything. Back to the log.
