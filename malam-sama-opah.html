Roblox Field Notes

Entry: MALAM SAMA OPAH

Genre: survival and escape. Studio: Sabun Studios. Players: up to 25.

The title is Malay for "A Night with Grandma", and the whole game takes place in Opah's house. Confusingly, the icon and thumbnails say Malam Sama Nenek instead. Nenek is just another Malay word for grandma, so videos under either name are the same game. Most of the in-game text is in Malay.

What you do

Choose a difficulty, Easy or Susah (Hard), and try to get out. Along the way you earn Duit, the in-game money, and spend it on spins for a class, called a KELAS. Classes you like can be kept in slots; slots three to five are sold as game passes.

Codes

One code is listed as active by a code site: SETTINGS, worth 3 normal spins and 500 Duit. It used to sit in the game description but was removed around 5 November, so treat it as possibly expired. The reported way to enter it is Settings at the bottom-right, then Codes on the left, then type and press Redeem. We have not clicked through those steps ourselves.
