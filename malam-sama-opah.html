Roblox Field Notes

Entry: MALAM SAMA OPAH

Genre: survival and escape. Studio: Sabun Studios. Players: up to 25. Released: 20 October 2026.

One game, two names

The Roblox title is MALAM SAMA OPAH, which is Malay for "A Night with Grandma". The icon, thumbnails and the in-game title card read MALAM SAMA NENEK instead. Nenek is just another Malay word for grandmother, so videos under either name are the same game. Most of the text is in Malay, with a few lines repeated in English, which is why many English searches spell it three different ways.

What the HUD tells you

The top-left corner shows three hearts, a small difficulty tag and an objective box. The objective reads "Cari 5 kunci, satu kod dan larikan diri dari rumah Opah", meaning find five keys, one code, and run away from Opah's house. Before the run you choose Easy or Susah, which means Hard. When Opah is close, hiding spots show a prompt to press E or click to leave, and in one clip the HUD drops to two of three hearts while the player hides.

The death screen hint

When the hearts run out, a black and red screen reads YOU DIED and then "Don't run in Opah's house." Under it are three grey hearts with the words OUT OF LIVES, and a note that you will watch the others from here. We have not seen a clip that proves exactly what running does, so we treat it as a hint and recommend walking. Our malam sama opah death screen page tracks this.

Classes, Duit and passes

Duit is the in-game money, earned by playing and spent on spins for a class, called a KELAS. Classes you like can be kept in slots, and slots three to five are sold as game passes. A DUIT x2 pass also exists. We have not seen what each class does, so we do not rank them. See malam sama opah classes for the current notes.

Codes

One code is listed as active by a code site: SETTINGS, worth three normal spins and 500 Duit. It used to sit in the game description but was removed around 5 November, so treat it as possibly expired. The reported way to enter it is Settings at the bottom-right, then Codes on the left, then type and press Redeem. We have not clicked through those steps ourselves.

The finish seems to happen outside: late footage shows the prompt RUN! GET ON THE BIKE! beside a motorbike. Details are on malam sama opah how to escape and the main malam sama opah page. Back to the log.

Quick tips from the footage

If you are hiding, the leave prompt is press E or click. If Susah is too much, Easy is always available. Keep an eye on the three hearts at the top left, because the death screen counts them. The house has green plank walls, red wooden floors, metal beds, wardrobes and a round table in the bedrooms, plus a bright tiled kitchen, so the key you carried through a bedroom was a green one.
