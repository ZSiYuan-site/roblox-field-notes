30 Floors Up lobby settings: the Create a Group window as a table

This log entry turns the lobby screen of 30 floors up roblox into a table, because the options are easier to compare side by side. Data checked Nov 9, 2026 from player videos; the matching long page is 30 floors up difficulty and lobbies.

How a group is made

The lobby is a large room with pads on the floor. A sign over an empty pad reads Available, step on to create a group, 1-4 players. Stepping on it opens the Create a Group window, labelled with the pad number. The green Create Group button sits at the bottom.

SettingChoicesWhat the window says
Players1, 2, 3 or 4Solo or up to three friends; lobby signs show counts like 4/4 and 2/3
DifficultyEasy, Normal, HardOnly Normal has text: the intended balance, recommended
AccessPublic, Friends onlyFriends only means only the host's Roblox friends can join
CampaignNew saved game, TemporarySaved starts your campaign; Temporary plays without saving
Save slotsSlot 2 and 3 are passes999 Robux each

Reading the table

Three things stand out. First, the only difficulty with a description is Normal, so there is no way to know from the footage how much harder Hard is. Second, Temporary is a trap for anyone who wants to continue later: a warning in the window says the progress is not saved. Third, the server itself holds up to 30 players, but a group tops out at 4, so most of the room is other people's groups.

Why a table and not a tier list

Lobby options are not ranked against each other; they are settings you combine. A table makes it obvious which cells are confirmed by the window text and which are blank. Where the cell says nothing, the footage said nothing. That is also why we do not claim to know how the Hard difficulty changes enemies, spawn counts or loot: no video we checked opens that detail, and guessing would only send you in expecting the wrong thing.
